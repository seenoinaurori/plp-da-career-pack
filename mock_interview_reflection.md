## Reflection prompts

After the interview, record specific evidence rather than filling these in from assumption:

- **What went well:** Which question did I answer clearly? What did the interviewer say or do that supports this?
- **What I will improve:** Where did I hesitate, use jargon, omit a caveat, or fail to explain my reasoning? Include one example.

## Concrete practice plan

1. **Before the mock:** Schedule a 20-minute call with a peer or mentor. Share the role context and ask them to use the five technical questions and two STAR prompts in [interview_prep.md](interview_prep.md).
2. **During the mock (20 minutes):** Spend 2 minutes on introductions, 10 minutes on technical questions, 6 minutes on behavioural questions, and 2 minutes on feedback. Ask the interviewer to note clarity, structure, accuracy, and pacing.
3. **Immediately after:** Write the actual interviewer name and date, capture one specific strength and one improvement area, and record any feedback in the reflection prompts above.
4. **Following week:** Practise the two weakest answers aloud twice, keeping each technical response under 90 seconds and each STAR story under 2 minutes. Re-run a timed mock and compare the feedback.
